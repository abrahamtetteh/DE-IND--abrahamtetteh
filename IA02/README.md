Client Interview Evidence and Requirement Transformation Memo1. Client Interview ContextClient Enterprise: Local traditional Ghanaian restaurant client providing local dishes (Banku, Waakye, Jollof Rice, Fufu).   Identified Problem: Manual order taking, phone calls, paper-based kitchen queuing, unrecorded custom meal requests, and lack of real-time inventory tracking.   2. Requirement Transformation MatrixRaw Statement 1: "All customer order taking, kitchen queuing, payment tracking, inventory management, and employee attendance logging are conducted manually." -> Functional Requirement (FR-01): The system shall provide an interactive menu, remote ordering checkout, automated delivery fee computation, POS/kitchen queuing, inventory stock tracking, MoMo/Cash payment logging, and staff attendance clock-in.   Raw Statement 2: "Inability to capture remote or off-site customer demand due to a lack of digital ordering mechanisms." -> Functional Requirement (FR-02): The web application shall allow remote customers to place orders supporting both self-pickup and delivery options.   Raw Statement 3: "Absence of real-time visibility into daily revenue, Cash vs. Mobile Money transaction breakdowns, and ingredient stock levels." -> Functional Requirement (FR-03): The administration dashboard shall render real-time sales analytics and ingredient safety threshold alerts.   3. User Stories and Acceptance CriteriaUser Story US-01: As a remote customer, I want to browse the menu and place an order online with automated delivery fee calculations so that I can receive traditional meals without manual phone calls.Acceptance Criteria: Given the customer selects items and inputs a delivery address, when checkout executes, then the distance-based delivery fee is computed and added to the subtotal.
